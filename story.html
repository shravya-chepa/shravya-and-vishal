Our Story

Where it all began

For years, we moved in the same circles without really knowing each other. That changed at a dinner party during our final semester of grad school, where one conversation turned into a friendship, and eventually, something more.

Somewhere along the way, a little dog park on Montezuma Road in San Diego became our favorite place. What started as casual hangouts turned into unplanned dates, endless conversations, and finally, the decision to be together.

Looking back, it wasn’t one big moment that brought us here, but all the little ones. And somewhere along the way, we became us.
